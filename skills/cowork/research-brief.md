description: "Structure research findings before saving them to the PRIME knowledge base. Use when you've done research and want to capture it cleanly as a note or article resource."
---

Help me structure this research into a clean brief for the PRIME knowledge base.

Format the output as a Markdown document with:

**Title** — specific enough to be findable in a search

**TL;DR** — two or three sentences. What's the core finding? What decision does this inform?

**Context** — why was this researched? Which PRIME project or decision does it connect to?

**Key findings** — the substance. Not a summary of sources, but the actual conclusions. Concrete over abstract. Include numbers, trade-offs, and specifics where available.

**Recommendation** — given the findings, what should PRIME do? If this is purely informational and doesn't drive a decision, say so.

**Sources** — list URLs or references. One line each.

**Tags** — 3–6 lowercase tags for the knowledge graph (e.g., `knowledge-graph`, `database`, `architecture`)

After drafting, flag anything that's missing, uncertain, or needs follow-up research before acting on this.
